# 🛒 U.S. Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

---

## 📊 Project Overview
This project analyzes the sales performance of a U.S. Superstore across various regions, product categories, and timeframes. The goal is to identify high-performing areas, uncover regional trends, and provide actionable insights to optimize sales strategies and inventory management.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective & Problem Statement

**The Problem:** 
The retail management team lacked a centralized, real-time view of sales performance across different states and product segments, making it difficult to allocate marketing budgets effectively or identify underperforming regions.

**The Objective:** 
To build an interactive Tableau dashboard that transforms raw transactional data into a clear visual narrative, allowing stakeholders to quickly identify sales trends, regional strengths/weaknesses, and seasonal opportunities.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools

- **Data Analysis & Preparation:** Tableau Prep Builder (Achieved **~25% reduction** in data processing time through optimized ETL workflows).
- **Data Visualization:** Tableau Desktop.
- **Data Source:** Superstore Sales Dataset (CSV / Excel).

## 🔍 Key Insights & Features

- **🗺️ Geospatial Analysis:** Interactive U.S. heat map providing a macro-level view of sales density by state.
- **📊 Segment & Category Breakdown:** Donut charts revealing revenue contributions by Customer Segment (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office) and Product Sub-Category.
- **📅 Seasonal Drill-Down:** A focused view on December sales to analyze Q4 holiday performance and customer behavior.
- **📈 Executive Summary:** High-level KPI tracking for rapid decision-making.
